Our Story

Every Spice Tells a Royal Story

At Zaika Mahal, we honor India's rich culinary traditions born in the grand royal kitchens of the Nawabs and Maharajas. Our master khansamas preserve centuries-old dum-pukht techniques, slow-cooking delicacies in sealed earthen deghs with fragrant saffron, cardamom, mace, and stone-ground spice blends. Every bite is an ode to authentic Indian royal heritage.
